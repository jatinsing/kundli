Privacy at a glance

Your birth information is used to generate astrological calculations. Kundli does not maintain its own server-side customer database of birth profiles, but information required for calculations is transmitted to an independent third-party astrology API.

Kundli may ask you to enter information required for astrological calculations, including a name or profile name, date of birth, time of birth, place, latitude, longitude, time zone, selected ayanamsha, and other calculation parameters.
